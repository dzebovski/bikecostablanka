Notes:
- Coll de Rates: smooth roads, steady ascent, the region's signature climb, the pro-team benchmark.
- Bernia: views to Calpe and Peñón de Ifach; technical descent with steep sections and tight hairpins ("take extra care").
- Vall d'Ebo: via the Pego valley, quiet inland roads, a balanced climbing day.

## Getting here
- Alicante (ALC) ~104 km, Valencia (VLC) ~111 km, almost all on the AP-7 (~1h–1h15 drive).

## Seasons
- Dec–Feb: pro cycling season, WorldTour teams train locally; mild sunny days; almond blossom Jan–Feb; Three Kings (Jan), carnival in Pego (Feb).
- Mar–Apr: orange blossom, sunny; Fallas in Dénia (March).
- Sep–Nov: warm, quiet beaches, relaxed pace.
- Jun–Sep: summer; Moors & Christians festivals.
